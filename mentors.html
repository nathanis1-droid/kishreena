The Program

About four hours,
over three months.

One young woman (18+) in India, introduced through our partner organisations. Everything happens on video.

Month 1 · week 1

Intro call

30 minutes. Get to know each other and agree on one goal. Book all your remaining dates before you hang up.

Month 1 · week 2–3

Session 1

1 hour of hands-on work on her goal, using your real expertise.

Month 2

Session 2

1 hour. Keep working on the goal and review what she's done since.

Month 3

Session 3

1 hour. Finish the work and write her plan together: three next steps with dates.

Two weeks later

Wrap-up

30 minutes. See how her plan is going and say a proper goodbye. If her goal isn't done, you can both choose to add up to three more sessions.

Before each call, we send you a short note with its goal and what to ask. No preparing from scratch.

Each mentee chooses one track and up to 3 things she wants help with. You tick up to 5 things you can do in a session, and we shortlist you for mentees whose picks match yours.

Safety Comes First

Six rules for every session.

Safety runs both ways: it protects the young woman, and it protects you.

1

One approved platform

All sessions on Kishreena's Google Meet. Never personal apps or WhatsApp.

2

Recorded as standard

Every session is recorded, with consent, and stored securely.

3

Kishreena email only

You get a Kishreena email address, and Kishreena is copied on emails. No personal contact details.

4

No money, ever

No money, gifts or financial requests, in either direction. This one is absolute.

5

Scheduled on Kishreena channels

You and your mentee agree times by Kishreena email, with Kishreena copied. Never privately.

6

Virtual only

No in-person meetings are arranged through Kishreena.

Code of Conduct

A guide, not a counsellor.

Every mentor completes a short training and signs our Code of Conduct before being matched. Here's what it asks of you.

You are here to…

  • Listen, encourage and believe in her
  • Share your own journey honestly, including the hard parts
  • Guide on career, direction and confidence
  • Show up on time and keep the dates you agree
  • Log each session and flag any concern

You are not here to…

  • Act as a therapist or counsellor
  • Solve personal or family problems
  • Give money or gifts, or make promises of jobs or referrals
  • Share personal contact details or social media
  • Push through anything that feels wrong

If something feels wrong

Anything at all, big or small, about her or about you, has one simple route. Reporting a concern is never overreacting.

1. NoticeSomething feels off, or she's in distress.
2. Respond kindlyStay warm. You don't have to handle it alone.
3. Tell usOne email to kishreenafoundation@gmail.com.
4. We follow upWe review it and route it to the right support.

Questions

Mentor FAQ

How much time does it really take?

About four hours over three months: a 30-minute intro, three 1-hour sessions and a 30-minute wrap-up, plus about 10 minutes of reading and notes around each session.

Who will my mentee be?

A young woman aged 18 or over in India, introduced through one of our partner organisations. Many are the first in their family to aim for a professional career. She chooses you from a shortlist of mentor profiles.

Do I need to prepare a curriculum?

No. Before each call we send you a short note with its goal and a few questions to ask. You bring your experience; we bring the structure.

Who schedules the calls?

You and your mentee agree times by Kishreena email, with Kishreena copied. Before you finish the intro call, book the dates for all three sessions and the wrap-up.

What if a session time stops working?

Email your mentee (with Kishreena copied) as early as you can and offer two new times. If anything gets stuck, email us and we'll help.

Can I help her get a job or internship?

Mentors offer guidance, not jobs, referrals or money. Please don't make promises. Belief in her and honest advice are what make the difference.

Where is the training?

After you sign up, we email you the training (about 20 minutes) and a short 5-question check. You'll be matched once both are done.
